## 1. Introduction to Computers
A **computer** is an electronic device that manipulates information or data. It has the ability to *store*, *retrieve*, and *process* data into useful information. 

Modern computing systems consist of both hardware (physical components) and software (instructions that tell the hardware what to do).

---

## 2. Types of Computers

Computers are broadly categorized based on their size, capability, and processing power:

* **Supercomputers:** Extremely fast machines designed to handle complex mathematical and scientific calculations.
* **Mainframe Computers:** Large-scale systems used by enterprise organizations for bulk data processing.
* **Minicomputers (Midrange):** Multi-user systems that lie between mainframes and microcomputers.
* **Microcomputers (Personal Computers):** Devices designed for individual use, including desktops and laptops.
